What We Do

Your Extended Legal Team, Working Remotely

We integrate directly into your workflow — not as a vendor, but as a reliable extension of your firm's operations.

Client Intake & Case Merit Evaluation

We handle intake calls, fact-checking, letters of representation, and HIPAA medical authorizations, so every new case starts on solid footing and nothing slips through in the first 48 hours.

Medical Records & Demand Packages

We retrieve and organize medical records, build chronologies and injury assessments, and structure demand packages that move negotiations forward rather than stalling during the review process.

Litigation & Discovery Support

Complaints, interrogatories, requests for production, subpoenas, and deposition summaries — drafted and organized so nothing catches your team off guard mid-case.

Mediation & Trial Prep

Exhibit binders, witness lists, mediation position papers, and settlement packages — assembled so your attorneys walk into every room already prepared.
